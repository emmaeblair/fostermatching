## 2. The 4 "Secret Ingredients" of Modern Color-Blocked UI

1. **Color Blocking Over Boring White Cards**:
   - Instead of 5 generic white cards with small colored text, the *surfaces themselves* carry bold, confident color blocks (rich periwinkle `#93B1FF`, butter yellow `#FFE685`, bubblegum pink `#FFD4E8`).
2. **Jet-Black Pill Anchor Buttons (`[ Match & Save ]`)**:
   - Notice how all 3 of your reference designs anchor high-energy pastel and electric colors with a solid **jet-black (`#141416`) pill button (`rounded-full`)**. This keeps the vibrant colors from feeling like a kindergarten toy and makes the app feel sleek, intentional, and modern.
3. **Squircle Geometry & Generous Padding**:
   - Containers use `rounded-[26px]` to `rounded-[32px]`. The extreme curvature makes cards feel organic, friendly, and touchable.
4. **Editorial Warmth Meets Clean System UI**:
   - Headlines use an expressive display font (*Fraunces* serif for pet names) to bring emotional connection, while badges and controls use ultra-clean modern sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).
