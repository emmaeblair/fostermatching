# Pop Art UI Style Guide for Adoption Matching

How to bring Roy Lichtenstein, Andy Warhol, and Keith Haring pop-art energy into our rescue pet matching app using the **Nude / Rose / Slate Grey / Lake Cyan / Salmon** palette (`#F1DEDE`, `#D496A7`, `#5D576B`, `#6CD4FF`, `#FE938C`).

---

## Why Pop Art Fits Pet Adoption

1. **Pets are inherently comic**: Head-tilts, goofy zoomies, barks, and dramatic cat naps are full of character. Pop Art frames them with the joy and drama they deserve.
2. **Instant Visual Hook**: Nobody in the pet adoption space does Pop Art. Standard apps are sterile blue/grey directories. A Pop Art approach turns your project into an unforgettable portfolio piece.
3. **Natural Graphic Structure**: Pop Art uses crisp, heavy ink outlines (`#5D576B`) and flat color blocks, which translates effortlessly into CSS borders, Flexbox containers, and responsive cards.

---

## Core Pop Art Design Elements to Use in Mockups

### 1. The Lichtenstein Starburst Match Badge (`💥 92% POW!`)
- Instead of a generic rounded pill badge for match percentage, use a **comic starburst / action burst**.
- Fill it with **Lake Cyan (`#6CD4FF`)** with a crisp 2px ink border and hard offset shadow.

### 2. The Comic Dialogue Balloon (Prompt Box)
- Shelter bios are usually written from a distance by volunteers. In our app, prompts are written in the pet's voice!
- Style the prompt card as a **literal comic speech bubble** with a small pointer tail directed at the dog's photo.

### 3. Ben-Day Halftone Dot Grid
- Soft, subtle halftone dots (created with CSS `radial-gradient`) in soft Rose (`#D496A7`) across the Nude canvas (`#F1DEDE`).
- Gives the app a tactile, silk-screened print texture without hurting contrast or readability.

### 4. Hard Offset Drop Shadows ("Neo-Brutalist Ink")
- Instead of blurry 2015 box-shadows, use sharp, hard drop shadows (`shadow-[3px_3px_0px_#5D576B]`).
- Buttons physically depress (`active:translate-x-[2px] active:translate-y-[2px] active:shadow-none`) like arcade buttons.

### 5. Typography Pairing
- **Display Headlines / Action Words**: **Bungee**, **Bangers**, or **Shrikhand** (punchy, retro comic-book feel).
- **Body & Decoded Badges**: **Fredoka** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (chunky, friendly, high legibility).
