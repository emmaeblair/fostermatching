# Coolors Palettes & 60-30-10 Implementation Review

This document breaks down the 5 candidate Coolors palettes, strictly mapped to the **60-30-10 UI rule** for Option 1 (Cozy Editorial / Hinge-style layout).

---

## The 60-30-10 Rules Applied to These Palettes

1. **60% (Canvas & Background)**: Must be very high lightness (`94%–98%`), softly tinted with the palette's warmest neutral so text remains razor sharp.
2. **30% (Cards, Prompt Containers & Structural Text)**:
   - Card surface = Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Prompt & secondary containers = Soft wash of the secondary warm tone (`88%–92%` lightness).
   - Main typography = The deepest tone in the palette (deep olive, slate, or charcoal) tinted with the brand hue rather than pure `#000000`.
3. **10% (Primary Action / CTA)**: The most vibrant warm accent (coral, peach, or grapefruit) reserved strictly for the **"Save & Match"** CTA and key active states.

---

## Detailed Palette Mapping

### 1. Palette 4: Peach / Butter / Celery / Sage / Olive Brown
- **Coolors Hex Codes**: `#F79F79` (Peach), `#F7D08A` (Butter), `#E3F09B` (Celery/Lime), `#87B6A7` (Sage), `#5B5941` (Deep Olive).
- **60% Canvas**: `#FBF8F1` (Soft butter/oat wash).
- **30% Surfaces & Text**: Card `#FFFFFF`, Prompt Container `#F7F1E1`, Text `#363527` (Deep olive brown—super warm and legible!).
- **10% Accent CTA**: `#F79F79` (Peach).
- **Badges**: `#87B6A7` (Sage Green for green flags) & `#E3F09B` (Celery for match score).
- **Vibe Assessment**: **Top Contender**. It feels outdoorsy, sunny, and natural. Olive brown text is much friendlier than harsh black.

---

### 2. Palette: Cerulean / Sea Green / Apricot / Cream / Grapefruit
- **Coolors Hex Codes**: `#227C9D` (Cerulean), `#17C3B2` (Sea Green), `#FFCB77` (Apricot), `#FEF9EF` (Warm Cream), `#FE6D73` (Grapefruit).
- **60% Canvas**: `#FEF9EF` (This palette literally includes the ideal warm cream canvas!).
- **30% Surfaces & Text**: Card `#FFFFFF`, Prompt Container `#FFF3DC` (Apricot wash), Text `#1A4150` (Deep cerulean-slate).
- **10% Accent CTA**: `#FE6D73` (Vibrant Grapefruit coral).
- **Badges**: `#17C3B2` (Crisp Sea Green for match scores and green flags).
- **Vibe Assessment**: **Strongest Built-in Balance**. Because `#FEF9EF` is already in the palette, the canvas looks completely native, and the sea green makes compatibility badges pop effortlessly.

---

### 3. Palette 5: Dark Slate / Ochre / Fiery Coral / Butter / Honeydew
- **Coolors Hex Codes**: `#352F3D` (Dark Plum Slate), `#D99438` (Ochre), `#FF6F4E` (Fiery Coral), `#FEE29C` (Butter), `#EEFDE6` (Honeydew Mint).
- **60% Canvas**: `#FDFBF7` (Ivory).
- **30% Surfaces & Text**: Card `#FFFFFF`, Prompt Container `#FFF1D4`, Text `#2E2734` (Deep plum slate—exceptional contrast).
- **10% Accent CTA**: `#FF6F4E` (Fiery Coral).
- **Badges**: `#EEFDE6` (Pale honeydew for positive flags).
- **Vibe Assessment**: **Most Confident & Modern**. The deep slate gives a premium magazine feel, and the coral button draws immediate focus.

---

### 4. Palette 3: Lilac / Mauve / Dusty Rose / Camel / Deep Slate
- **Coolors Hex Codes**: `#E3D8F1` (Lilac), `#DABECA` (Mauve), `#BF8B85` (Dusty Rose), `#AD8A64` (Camel), `#5D5F71` (Slate).
- **60% Canvas**: `#F8F5FA` (Soft lilac-tinted white).
- **30% Surfaces & Text**: Card `#FFFFFF`, Prompt Container `#F3ECF6`, Text `#363645` (Slate).
- **10% Accent CTA**: `#BF8B85` (Dusty Rose).
- **Badges**: `#AD8A64` (Warm camel leather badge) & `#E3D8F1` (Lilac).
- **Vibe Assessment**: **Boutique & Calming**. Lower sensory stimulation, feels like a cozy, high-end pet journal.

---

### 5. Palette: Nude / Rose / Slate Grey / Lake Cyan / Salmon
- **Coolors Hex Codes**: `#F1DEDE` (Blush Nude), `#D496A7` (Rose), `#5D576B` (Plum Slate), `#6CD4FF` (Lake Cyan), `#FE938C` (Salmon).
- **60% Canvas**: `#FAF5F5` (Soft blush wash).
- **30% Surfaces & Text**: Card `#FFFFFF`, Prompt Container `#F7ECEE`, Text `#322E3B` (Plum Slate).
- **10% Accent CTA**: `#FE938C` (Salmon).
- **Badges**: `#6CD4FF` (Lake cyan brings an upbeat splash of water/sky for match scores).
- **Vibe Assessment**: **Playful & Youthful**. The unexpected pop of lake cyan keeps the pinks from feeling too monochromatic.
