# Adopter Personas & Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

Because this app focuses strictly on the **Adopter experience** (relying on APIs for pet inventory and linking out to rescue applications), our personas represent different types of pet seekers frustrated by standard listing directories.

---

## Persona 1: "The Overwhelmed First-Time Adopter"
- **Context**: Lives in an apartment or townhome, works hybrid/remote, wants a cat or dog companion, and has been casually scrolling Petfinder at night.
- **Frustrations (Pains)**:
  - Endless grids of photos where every bio requires clicking in and reading 4 paragraphs of text to find out if the dog barks at neighbors or hates stairs.
  - Standard filters only cover basics (size, breed, age) and miss *vibe* or energy level.
  - Seeing pets marked "pending" or already adopted.
- **Goals (Gains)**:
  - A fun, low-stress way to browse strictly **available** pets.
  - Instantly see extracted compatibility highlights (e.g., *Couch Potato*, *Apartment Friendly*, *Shy at First*) without reading a wall of text.
- **Job-to-be-Done**:
  - *When I* want to adopt a pet that fits my apartment and work routine, *I want to* browse available animals matched to my lifestyle in an engaging format, *so I can* confidently click through to apply for a pet that actually fits my life.

---

## Persona 2: "The Specific-Constraints Household"
- **Context**: Already has a cat (or young kids, or a specific yard setup) and wants to add a rescue dog or second cat without risking a bad fit.
- **Frustrations (Pains)**:
  - Shelter database checkboxes for "Good with cats" are frequently left blank by shelter staff even when the bio text mentions how the animal acts around other pets.
  - Having to open 30 browser tabs to compare potential matches.
- **Goals (Gains)**:
  - Smarter filtering and matching that catches details buried in bios.
  - A clean "Saved / Top Matches" list where they can compare favorites with their partner and jump straight to the rescue's application link.
- **Job-to-be-Done**:
  - *When I* am searching for a pet that can safely live with my family/existing pets, *I want* intelligent filtering and clear match explanations, *so I can* save time and apply only for animals that meet our household needs.
